D5: Surface Cleaning and Waste Bag Replacement

Frequency: Daily

Time: 10-15 minutes

Videos: Google Drive Folder

Materials Needed

  • Cleaning cloth
  • Food-safe surface sanitizer
  • New coffee pond trash bag
  • New trash bag

Procedure

Step 1: Surface Cleaning

  1. Wipe all external machine surfaces.
  2. Clean around the beverage outlet area.
  3. Clean any spills, milk residue, powder, or coffee stains.
  4. Make sure the customer-facing area looks clean.

Step 2: Swap the Coffee Pond Trash Bag

  1. Open the coffee pond compartment.
  2. Remove the used coffee grounds trash bag carefully.
  3. Tie the bag securely and dispose of it.
  4. Install a new coffee pond trash bag.
  5. Make sure the bag is open and properly fitted.

Step 3: Swap the Trash Bag

  1. Remove the used trash bag from the trash bin.
  2. Tie the bag securely and dispose of it.
  3. Install a new trash bag.
  4. Wipe the trash bin rim if needed.

Important Warnings

⚠️ Do NOT leave coffee grounds overflowing.

⚠️ Make sure new bags are installed correctly before closing.

⚠️ Clean any spills immediately to avoid odor and pests.

⚠️ Report broken bin, leaking bag, or strong odor immediately.
