Recalibrate the Scales

Calibrate milk and water dispensing accuracy

Purpose
Ensure accurate dispensing of milk and water. Prevents over/under-portioning that affects drink quality and consistency.
📄 SOP Steps
  • Step 1 Open the CoffeeRobot Console on the robot PC
  • Step 2 Select Milk from the menu

    → Follow the on-screen calibration steps one by one

    → Complete all steps until calibration is confirmed
  • Step 3 Select Water from the menu

    → Follow the on-screen calibration steps one by one

    → Complete all steps until calibration is confirmed
⚠️ Follow all on-screen prompts carefully. Do not skip any steps during calibration.
