W4 — Weekly

Clean Coffee Grinder

Use GRINDZ tablets to clean grinder burrs and remove oil buildup

Purpose
Remove coffee oil and residue from grinder burrs to maintain grind quality and prevent stale flavour buildup.
🧴 Required Supplies
🟡
Urnex GRINDZ — Grinder Cleaner
Food-safe, gluten-free & organic grinder cleaning tablets. Absorbs coffee oils and residue as they pass through the burrs.
✅ Gluten Free & Organic
📄 SOP Steps
  • Step 1 Prepare the Urnex GRINDZ grinder cleaner tablets — have them ready before starting.
  • Step 2 On the coffee machine screen, tap 维护保养 (Maintenance), then select 磨豆机保养 (Grinder Maintenance).
  • Step 3 Follow the on-screen prompts step by step until the cleaning cycle is complete. ⚠️ Do not skip or interrupt steps mid-cycle — follow the screen exactly.
