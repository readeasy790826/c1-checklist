Tea Brewing SOP

Dianmood — Pre-brewed tea preparation procedure

茉莉花茶 Jasmine Green Tea Premium · Tapio ▶
IngredientAmount
Tea leaves50 g
Hot water (80°C)1500 ml
Ice500 g
Steps
1
Add 50 g tea leaves to the brewing container.
2
Pour in 1500 ml 80°C hot water, close the lid, and steep for 7 minutes.
3
Prepare a separate container with 500 g ice.
4
Before the timer ends, strain the tea into the container with ice.
5
Close the lid and seal with plastic wrap. Brewing complete.
红茶 Assam Black Tea Premium · Tapio ▶
IngredientAmount
Tea leaves70 g
Hot water (100°C)1500 ml
Ice500 g
Steps
1
Add 70 g tea leaves to the brewing container.
2
Pour in 1500 ml 100°C hot water, close the lid, and steep for 12 minutes.
3
Prepare a separate container with 500 g ice.
4
When the timer ends, strain the tea twice into the container with ice and mix.
5
Close the lid and seal with plastic wrap. Brewing complete.
抹茶 Matcha ALIGN Grade B Barista · ICHA Mocha ▶
IngredientAmount
Matcha powder50 g
Warm water (50°C)200 ml
Chilled water (fridge)1300 ml
步骤
1
将 50 g 抹茶粉过筛至抹茶容器。
2
倒入 200 ml 50°C 温水，充分搅拌至粉末完全融合。
3
加入 1300 ml 冷藏冰水，搅拌混合均匀。 ⚠️ 用冷藏冰水（非冰块）确保总量精准，浓度稳定在 1:30。
4
盖好盖子，保鲜膜密封。粹茶完成。
