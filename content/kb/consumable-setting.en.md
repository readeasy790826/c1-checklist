title: Consumable Setting
---

**Purpose:** Measure how much the coffee machine actually dispenses, then enter that amount into Consumable Setting so COGS tracking stays accurate.

# Syrups

1. Prepare a measuring cup.

![Measuring cup](assets/consumable_measuring_cup.jpeg)

2. Place the measuring cup under the coffee machine’s syrup spout.
3. On CoffeeRobot Console, open **Device → Coffee Machine → Syrup Dispenser Settings**.

![Syrup Dispenser Settings location](assets/console_syrup_setting_location.jpeg)

4. Find the syrup row you want to measure (example: **ID 32 – Syrup 4 Long**). Press the **▶** on that row. Syrup will dispense into the measuring cup.

![Syrup Dispenser Settings](assets/syrup_dispenser_settings.jpeg)

5. Read the amount of syrup in the measuring cup.
6. Go to **COGS MANAGEMENT → Consumables Setting →** (example) **syrup 4**. Find **Drink ID 32** and enter that amount into **Consumption**, then save.

![Consumables Setting](assets/consumable_setting_syrup.jpeg)

7. Repeat for the other settings as needed.
