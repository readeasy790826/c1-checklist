---
title: Ingredient Restocking SOP
---

> [!WARNING] Complete before starting
> Keep all ingredients sealed until you reach the machine, and put on gloves first.
> Open the keyboard, press **ESC**, go to [admin.hestiarobotics.com](https://admin.hestiarobotics.com/) and set the system to **In Maintenance** mode.

# 1. Fridge 1 — Matcha / Milk / Oat Milk

1. Open Fridge 1 and turn off the matcha rotator.
2. Disconnect the tube and remove the old matcha canister.
3. Install the new matcha canister and reconnect the tube.
4. Turn the rotator knob to the set position; confirm the matcha is rotating with **no foaming**.
5. Replace the milk, then the oat milk, with fresh stock.
6. Make sure every tubing connector is **firmly tightened**.
7. Close the fridge.

# 2. Fridge 2 — Tea Liquid

1. Open Fridge 2, remove the old tea liquid, load fresh stock, and close the fridge.
2. Take out the machine keyboard and open the system interface.
3. Tap **COGS MANAGEMENT** → find **Blacktea** → update the added tea volume (ml).
4. Tap **Device Monitor** → tap **Flavor Dispenser #1** → enter **8** and tap submit.
5. Confirm the tea dispenses smoothly.

# 3. Coffee Beans

1. Pour the beans into the hopper and close the lid.
2. Take out the keyboard and open the system interface.
3. Tap **COGS MANAGEMENT** → find **Coffee beans** → update the new total weight.

# 4. Chocolate Powder

1. Pour the chocolate powder into the hopper and close the lid.
2. Take out the keyboard and open the system interface.
3. Tap **COGS MANAGEMENT** → find **Chocolate Powder** → update the new total weight.

# 5. Cups

1. Load the cups into the cup dropper and close the lid.
2. Take out the keyboard and open the system interface.
3. Tap **COGS MANAGEMENT** → find the matching dropper **cup#1** or **cup#2** → update the new total weight.

# 6. Syrups

1. Remove the near-empty syrup and install a new one.
2. Take out the keyboard and open the system interface.
3. Tap **COGS MANAGEMENT** → find the matching syrup number → update the new total weight.

# After You Finish

> [!TIP] Must do before leaving
> Go to [admin.hestiarobotics.com](https://admin.hestiarobotics.com/) and set the system back to **Online** mode.
> On the CoffeeRobot Console, double-click the blank area of the KDS to return to the ad screen, then put the keyboard back.
