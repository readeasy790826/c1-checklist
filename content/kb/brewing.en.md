# Jasmine Green Tea

*Premium · Tapio*

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Tea leaves | 50 g |
| Hot water (80°C) | 1500 ml |
| Ice | 500 g |

1. Add 50 g tea leaves to the brewing container.
2. Pour in 1500 ml **80°C** hot water, close the lid, and steep for **7 minutes**.
3. Prepare a separate container with 500 g ice.
4. Before the timer ends, strain the tea into the container with ice.
5. Close the lid and seal with plastic wrap. Brewing complete.

# Assam Black Tea

*Premium · Tapio*

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Tea leaves | 70 g |
| Hot water (100°C) | 1500 ml |
| Ice | 500 g |

1. Add 70 g tea leaves to the brewing container.
2. Pour in 1500 ml **100°C** hot water, close the lid, and steep for **12 minutes**.
3. Prepare a separate container with 500 g ice.
4. When the timer ends, strain the tea **twice** into the container with ice and mix.
5. Close the lid and seal with plastic wrap. Brewing complete.

# Matcha

*ALIGN Grade B Barista · ICHA Mocha*

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Matcha powder | 50 g |
| Warm water (50°C) | 200 ml |
| Chilled water (fridge) | 1300 ml |

1. **Sift** 50 g matcha powder into the matcha container.
2. Add 200 ml **50°C** warm water and stir until the powder is fully blended.
3. Add 1300 ml chilled water and stir until evenly mixed.
4. Close the lid and seal with plastic wrap. Brewing complete.

> [!NOTE] Use chilled water (not ice cubes) so the total volume stays exact and the concentration holds steady at 1:30.

# Fructose Syrup

*Sugar syrup · Water*

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Fructose syrup | 500 ml |
| Water | 100 ml |

![Fong Leng fructose](assets/fructose_syrup.jpg)

1. In a graduated jar, pour **500 ml** fructose syrup, add **100 ml** water, and stir thoroughly.
2. Pour the blended syrup into the dedicated syrup bottle and cap it for later use.
