title: Machine Moving SOP
---

*Pre-move preparation checklist — complete every step before relocating the machine.*

# 1. Coffee Machine Wash & Boiler Cooldown

1. Run the machine's full cleaning cycle (Tablet Cleaning).
2. When cleaning finishes, **switch off the coffee machine** and let the boiler cool naturally.
3. Wait at least **60 minutes** and confirm the body is no longer warm before moving it.

> [!DANGER] Never move the machine before the boiler has cooled — risk of burns and internal damage.

# 2. Ice Machine — Hold Clean to Drain

1. Find the **Clean** button on the ice machine control panel.
2. **Press and hold Clean** to enter drain / anti-leak mode.
3. Wait for the machine to finish draining; confirm no standing water before continuing.

> [!TIP] This step stops internal water from overflowing during the move — it must be done before relocating.

# 3. Dispense All Ice from Ice Machine

1. Dispense all the ice out of the machine.
2. Confirm the ice bin is **completely empty**, with no leftover cubes.

> [!DANGER] Leftover ice melts during the move, causing leaks and a risk of electrical short circuits.

# 4. Remove Robotic Arm

1. Confirm the machine is powered off.
2. **Remove the robotic arm** following the standard procedure and set it aside safely to avoid knocks.
3. Check there are no leftover parts at the arm's connection point before moving the whole unit.

> [!TIP] Pack and transport the robotic arm separately to prevent damage during the move.
