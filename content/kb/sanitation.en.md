---
title: Container Cleaning & Sanitising SOP
---

> [!NOTE] Bleach dilution standard: **1 ml bleach + 1 L water = 50 ppm**

> [!INFO] **Cleaner rules**
> Milk-line cleaning tablet / Urnex Rinza → milk fat & protein (alkaline) → **milk jug only**
> Citric acid → tea polyphenols & tannins (acidic) → **tea urn only**
> ⚠️ The two must never be swapped.

# Milk Jug

## 1. Milk change / basic wash

1. Empty it completely — never mix old and new milk; discard any leftover milk.
2. Pre-rinse with **45°C** warm water to wash off the milk-fat film on the walls.
3. Add **warm water + a milk-line cleaning tablet** and soak for **10 minutes**.
4. Lightly scrub the walls and bottom corners with a soft brush or sponge.
5. Rinse with clean water **at least twice**.

## 2. Sanitise

1. Mix bleach solution to **50 ppm** (1 ml bleach + 1 L water).
2. Soak for **10 minutes**.
3. Pour out and let drain naturally.

## 3. Dry & store

1. Invert on a ventilated rack; only put the lid on once fully dry.
2. **Never seal the lid with moisture still inside.**

# Stainless Steel Tea Urn

> [!WARNING] Tea liquid keeps for no more than 24 hours.

## 1. Wash

1. Empty the tea liquid.
2. Rinse with **45°C** warm water (prevents the tannin film from browning on the walls).
3. Add **warm water + citric acid** (5 g citric acid + 1 L warm water) and soak for **10 minutes**.
4. Clean the walls, bottom corners, and inside of the lid with a sponge or soft brush.
5. Rinse with clean water **twice**.

## 2. Sanitise

1. Mix bleach solution to **50 ppm**.
2. Soak for **10 minutes**.
3. Pour out and let drain naturally.

# Water Bucket Cover

1. Wash with warm water.
2. Soak in bleach solution for **10 minutes** to sanitise.
