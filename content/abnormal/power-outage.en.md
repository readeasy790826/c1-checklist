---
title: Power Outage Operation Steps
---

1. Press the restart button on the EcoFlow power unit’s socket to reboot the power supply.
2. Long-press the power button of the robotic arm control host to power on the robotic arm.
3. After the computer restarts, launch the app and run console-restart.
4. If the power outage lasts more than two hours:
   - Discard all raw materials stored in the refrigerator and perform a deep cleaning.
5. If the power outage lasts less than two hours:
   - Inspect the raw materials for spoilage.
   - If no spoilage is found — business operations may resume normally.
   - If any spoilage is detected — follow the handling rules for outages exceeding two hours.
