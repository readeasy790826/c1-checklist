**Purpose:** Remove accumulated coffee and milk residue from spout groups to maintain hygiene and prevent blockages.

## SOP Steps

1. Take off the spout group and clean it as shown in the reference video.
2. If it is too difficult to remove, use a screwdriver as shown in the reference video.
   - Tip: Use the screwdriver gently to avoid damaging the spout group or surrounding parts.
