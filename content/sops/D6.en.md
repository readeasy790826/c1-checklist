## Materials Needed

- Keyboard

## Procedure

### Step 1: Set Robot Status Back to Online

1. Open **Microsoft Edge** browser.
2. Log into [admin.hestiarobotics.com](https://admin.hestiarobotics.com/).
3. Click **Robots**.
4. Find the correct robot.
5. Change the robot status from **"in maintenance"** to **"Online"**.
6. Confirm the robot status shows **Online**.

### Step 2: Set KDS to Full Screen Mode

1. Open **CoffeeRobot Console**.
2. Click **KDS**.
3. Double-click the blank area in the middle of the KDS screen.
4. Confirm KDS enters **full screen mode**.
5. Confirm advertisements appear on the screen.

> Do NOT set the robot Online until D1–D5 are fully completed.
> Make sure KDS is full screen before leaving the machine.
> Make sure advertisements are showing on the screen.
> If the robot cannot be set Online, contact supervisor immediately.
