**Purpose:** Clear memory buildup and prevent the kiosk app from freezing or becoming unresponsive over time.

## SOP Steps

1. **Power off the kiosk** — Unplug the kiosk power cable completely.
2. **Wait 30 seconds** — Leave it unpowered for at least 30 seconds to fully discharge.
3. **Power on the kiosk** — Plug the power cable back in and wait for the kiosk to fully boot up.
4. **Restart the kiosk app** — Once the kiosk has booted, relaunch / restart the kiosk app and confirm it loads correctly.

> Make sure the kiosk app is fully loaded and displaying correctly before leaving.
