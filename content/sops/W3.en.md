**Purpose:** Remove coffee residue buildup to maintain brew quality and prevent blockages.

## SOP Steps

1. Follow the video to remove the brewer.
2. Install the replacement brewer.
3. Test one cup of coffee — confirm normal output with no errors.
4. Clean the removed brewer:
   - Brush off coffee powder residue.
   - Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
   - Allow to dry completely before storing.
