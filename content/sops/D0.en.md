## Materials Needed

- Keyboard

## Procedure

### Step 1: Enter Maintenance Mode

1. Turn on keyboard power.
2. Press **Esc** key to exit advertising screen.
3. Click **Microsoft Edge** browser to enter web interface.
4. Log into [admin.hestiarobotics.com](https://admin.hestiarobotics.com/).
5. Click **Robots** and change the status to **"in maintenance"**.

> Make sure the correct robot is selected before changing status.
> Do NOT start cleaning before the robot status is changed to "in maintenance".
> Keep the robot in maintenance mode until D1–D5 are completed.
