**Purpose:** Empty and run the ice maker's cleaning cycle to clear scale and residue buildup and keep ice production hygienic.

## SOP Steps

1. Disconnect the water inlet.
2. Press and hold the cleaning button (about 10 seconds) to start the cleaning cycle.
3. Wait until the machine enters standby mode.
