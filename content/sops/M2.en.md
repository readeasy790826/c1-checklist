**Purpose:** Remove powder residue and buildup from internal components to prevent clogs and maintain consistent product quality.

## SOP Steps

1. Disassemble the powder components following the reference video.
2. Wash all components thoroughly and allow to dry completely.
3. Reinstall all components once fully dry.
