---
title: Syrup Dispenser Deep Cleaning
freq: monthly
time: 10–15 min
---

**Purpose:** Remove syrup residue from tubes to prevent clogging, maintain flavor accuracy, and ensure hygienic dispensing.

> Wear gloves before handling syrup tubes.

## SOP Steps

1. Put on gloves.
2. Pull the syrup tube out of the syrup bottle.
3. Fill a cup with warm water and place the syrup tube into the cup.
4. Open CoffeeRobot Console → `Coffee Machine` → `Syrup Dispenser Settings`. Click the **▶ arrow** next to the corresponding syrup to start flushing. Run until the water in the tube runs clear.
