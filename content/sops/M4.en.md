title: Coffee Grinder Cleaning
freq: monthly
time: 15 min
---

**Purpose:** Remove coffee oil and fine residue from grinder burrs to maintain grind quality and prevent flavour contamination.

## Materials Needed

![Urnex GRINDZ — Grinder Cleaner, Gluten Free & Organic, NET 430g (15.2 oz)](assets/grindz_cleaner.jpg)

**Urnex GRINDZ™** — Grinder Cleaner. Gluten Free & Organic. NET 430g (15.2 oz). Food safe.

## SOP Steps

1. On the coffee machine screen, tap `Maintenance` → `Grinder Maintenance`.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions step by step to complete the operation.
   - Use Urnex GRINDZ™ cleaning tablets during the process, following the on-screen instructions.
