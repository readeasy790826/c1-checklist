title: Descaling the coffee machine water system
freq: yearly
---

**Purpose:** Descale the coffee machine water system to clear mineral buildup.

## Materials Needed

**Urnex DEZCAL** — 100g in 2L warm water (30–40°C).

![Urnex DEZCAL](assets/y1_dezcal.jpg)

> [!IMPORTANT] Descaling agents are highly toxic. All containers or tubes shall be thoroughly rinsed after use.

## SOP Steps

1. Grab a container which is dedicated to descaling, at least 2 liters.
![Descaling container](assets/y1_descaling_container.jpg)
2. Put 100g DEZCAL into 2L warm water (30–40°C).
3. Run the descaling program on the coffee machine: go to **Maintenance settings → Descaling**.
4. Follow the instructions step by step.
