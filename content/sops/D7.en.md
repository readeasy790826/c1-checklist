**Purpose:** Ensure the chocolate powder hopper is correctly filled, free of clumping, and no product remains stuck to the walls — preventing dispensing errors and inconsistent drink output.

## SOP Steps

1. Open the chocolate powder hopper compartment.
2. Inspect the powder level and check the interior walls for any product stuck to the sides or corners.
3. Level the powder inside using a clean, dry utensil — gently sweep any stuck powder off the walls and redistribute evenly.
   - Do not use wet or damp tools inside the hopper.
4. Confirm no product remains stuck to the walls and the surface is level.
5. Close the hopper compartment securely.
