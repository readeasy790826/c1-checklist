## Materials Needed

Prepare **3 bottles** before starting:

1. **Rinza Milk Frother Cleaning Tablet Water** — Add **2 Rinza Milk Frother Cleaning Tablets** into 500ml warm water (40–60°C)
2. **100ppm Bleach Sanitizer**
3. **Clean Water**

![Rinza Milk Frother Cleaning Tablet](assets/d3_rinza_milk_frother_tablet.jpg)

![Three Bottles for D3 Cleaning](assets/d3_three_bottles.jpg)

## SOP Steps

### Step 1: Tablet Cleaning of Milk System

1. Unplug the milk tubes in Fridge#1, connect the extension tubes and insert tubes into **Milk System Cleaning Tablet Water** bottle
2. Coffee Machine → Maintenance → Tablet Cleaning of Milk System
3. Follow the instructions on the screen step by step

### Step 2: Bleach Sanitizer Cycle

1. Insert tubes into **100ppm Bleach Sanitizer** bottle
2. Coffee Machine → Back → Main Screen → Milk1 After Clean, Milk2 After Clean, Milk3 After Clean

### Step 3: Milk System Rapid Rinse Cycle

1. Insert tubes into **Clean Water** bottle
2. Coffee Machine → Back → Press the three-line menu button on the upper left → Milk System Rapid Rinse Cycle
3. Follow the instructions on the screen step by step

### Step 4: Pump the milk liquid to the dispenser outlet

1. Plug the tubes back to the milk containers
2. Coffee Machine → Back → Main Screen → Milk1 After Clean, Milk2 After Clean, Milk3 After Clean
3. Make sure to see milk liquid dispensed through the beverage spout groups

> Use bottles in correct order — Milk Cleaning Tablet → Bleach Sanitizer → Clean Water
> Do NOT skip bleach sanitizer — Required for food safety
> Complete all rinse cycles — Ensure no chemical residue
> Make sure to see milk liquid dispensed through the beverage spout groups to successfully complete this procedure
