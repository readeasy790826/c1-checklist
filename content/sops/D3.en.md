## Materials Needed

Prepare **2 bottles** before starting:

1. **Rinza Milk Frother Cleaning Tablet Water** — Add **2 Rinza Milk Frother Cleaning Tablets** into 500ml warm water (40–50°C)
2. **Clean Water**

![Rinza Milk Frother Cleaning Tablet](assets/d3_rinza_milk_frother_tablet.jpg)

## SOP Steps

### Step 1: Tablet Cleaning of Milk System

1. Make sure water for coffee machine is above 3L to finish the milk system cleaning.
2. Unplug the milk tubes in Fridge#1, connect the extension tubes and insert tubes into **Milk System Cleaning Tablet Water** bottle.
3. Return to coffee machine interface, click **top-right "≡"** (three lines) to enter Quick Menu Page.
4. Enter password when prompted (date/time format, numbers reversed).
5. Click and hold the **"Flushing of Milk System"** for 8 seconds until the **"Tablet Cleaning of Milk System"** appears.
6. Follow the instructions on the screen step by step.

### Step 2: Milk System Rapid Rinse Cycle

1. Insert tubes into **Clean Water** bottle.
2. Return to coffee machine interface, click **top-right "≡"** (three lines) to enter Quick Menu Page.
3. Enter password when prompted (date/time format, numbers reversed).
4. Click **"Flushing of Milk System"**.
5. {red}Just click "Confirm" and "Confirm", ignore the instruction on the screen.{/red}

### Step 3: Pump the milk liquid to the dispenser outlet

1. Plug the tubes back to the milk containers
2. Coffee Machine → Back → Main Screen → Milk1 After Clean, Milk2 After Clean, Milk3 After Clean
3. Make sure to see milk liquid dispensed through the beverage spout groups

> Use bottles in correct order — Milk Cleaning Tablet → Clean Water
> Complete all rinse cycles — Ensure no chemical residue
> Make sure to see milk liquid dispensed through the beverage spout groups to successfully complete this procedure
