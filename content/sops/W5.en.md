---
title: Recalibrate the Scales
freq: weekly
time: 10 min
---

**Purpose:** Ensure accurate dispensing of milk and water. Prevents over/under-portioning that affects drink quality and consistency.

## SOP Steps

1. Open the **CoffeeRobot Console** on the robot PC.
2. Select **Milk** from the menu.
   - Follow the on-screen calibration steps one by one.
   - Complete all steps until calibration is confirmed.
3. Select **Water** from the menu.
   - Follow the on-screen calibration steps one by one.
   - Complete all steps until calibration is confirmed.

> Follow all on-screen prompts carefully. Do not skip any steps during calibration.
