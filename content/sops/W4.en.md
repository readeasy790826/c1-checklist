title: Clean the Fridge
freq: weekly
time: 15–20 min
updated: 2026-06-07
version: 1.0
---

**Purpose:** Prevent bacteria buildup and maintain food safety standards for all stored ingredients.

## SOP Steps

1. Remove all items from the fridge.
2. Check expiry dates on all ingredients — discard any expired products.
3. Wipe down the interior walls, shelves, and door seals with a damp cloth and food-safe cleaner.
4. Wipe again with a clean damp cloth to remove any cleaner residue.
5. Dry thoroughly, then return all ingredients neatly.
