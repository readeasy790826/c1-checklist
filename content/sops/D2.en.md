## Materials Needed

Prepare **3 bottles** before starting:

1. **Citric Acid Water** — 1L fresh water + 5g Citric Acid
2. **100ppm Bleach Sanitizer**
3. **Clean Water**

![Citric Acid](assets/d2_citric_acid.jpg)

![Three Bottles for D2 Cleaning](assets/d2_three_bottles.jpg)

## SOP Steps

### Step 1: Find Daily Milk Cleaning on CoffeeRobot Console

CoffeeRobot Console → Device → Flavor Dispenser#1 → Daily Milk Cleaning

![CoffeeRobot Console - Device Monitor](assets/d2_console_device_monitor.jpg)

![CoffeeRobot Console - Daily Milk Cleaning](assets/d2_console_daily_milk_cleaning.jpg)

### Step 2: Citric Acid Cleaning Cycle

1. Unplug the tubes in Fridge#2 and insert tubes into **Citric Acid Water** bottle
2. Daily Milk Cleaning — Click OK
3. Citric acid water is dispensed through the flavor dispenser outlet

### Step 3: Clean Water Cycle

1. Insert tubes into **Clean Water** bottle
2. Daily Milk Cleaning — Click OK
3. Clean Water is dispensed through the flavor dispenser outlet

### Step 4: Bleach Sanitizer Cycle

1. Insert tubes into **100ppm Bleach Sanitizer** bottle
2. Daily Milk Cleaning — Click OK
3. 100ppm Bleach Sanitizer is dispensed through the flavor dispenser outlet

### Step 5: Clean Water Rinse Cycle

1. Insert tubes into **Clean Water** bottle
2. Daily Milk Cleaning — Click OK
3. Clean Water is dispensed through the flavor dispenser outlet

### Step 6: Pump the tea liquid to the dispenser outlet

1. Plug the tubes to the containers
2. Drink ID on device — input "8" — submit
3. Make sure to see tea liquid dispensed through the flavor dispenser outlet

> Use bottles in correct order — Citric Acid → Clean Water → Bleach Sanitizer → Clean Water
> Do NOT skip bleach sanitizer — Required for food safety
> Complete all rinse cycles — Ensure no chemical residue
> Make sure to see tea liquid dispensed through the flavor dispenser outlet to successfully complete this procedure
