Syrup Tube Flushing

Flush syrup tubes to prevent residue buildup and clogging

Purpose
Remove syrup residue from tubes to prevent clogging, maintain flavor accuracy, and ensure hygienic dispensing.
📄 SOP Steps
⚠️ Wear gloves before handling syrup tubes.
  • Step 1 Put on gloves
  • Step 2 Pull the syrup tube out of the syrup bottle
  • Step 3 Fill a cup with warm water and place the syrup tube into the cup
  • Step 4 Open CoffeeRobot Console → Coffee Machine → Syrup:
    Coffee Machine → Syrup
    Click the ▶ arrow next to the corresponding syrup to start flushing. Run until the water in the tube runs clear
