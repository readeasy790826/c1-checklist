Coffee Grinder Cleaning

Clean coffee grinder using cleaning tablets

Purpose
Remove coffee oil and fine residue from grinder burrs to maintain grind quality and prevent flavour contamination.
🧴 Required Supply
Urnex GRINDZ™
Grinder Cleaner — Gluten Free & Organic
NET 430g (15.2 oz.)
✅ Food Safe
📄 SOP Steps
  • Step 1 On the coffee machine screen, tap Maintenance → Grinder Maintenance
  • Step 2 Follow the on-screen instructions step by step to complete the operation
    ⚠️ Use Urnex GRINDZ™ cleaning tablets during the process, following the on-screen instructions
