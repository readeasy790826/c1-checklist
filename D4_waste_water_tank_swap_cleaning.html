D4: Waste Water Tank Swap and Cleaning

Frequency: Daily

Time: 10 minutes

Videos: Google Drive Folder

Materials Needed

  • Empty waste water tank / clean replacement tank

Procedure

Step 1: Remove the Used Waste Water Tank

  1. Tighten the red cap.
  2. Take one stopper.
  3. Carefully pull out the waste water tube and plug it with the stopper.
  4. Tighten the white cap.
  5. Pull out the waste water tank.

Step 2: Install the Empty Waste Water Tank

  1. Place the empty waste water tank into the machine.
  2. Unscrew the white cap and place it aside.
  3. Remove the stopper and insert the waste water tube into the tank.
  4. Loosen the red cap as much as possible.

Step 3: Empty, Clean, and Sanitize the Used Waste Water Tank

  1. Carry the used waste water tank carefully to the sink or designated drain.
  2. Pour out all waste water.
  3. Rinse the tank thoroughly with clean water.
  4. Wash the inside and outside of the tank if there is any residue or smell.
  5. Sanitize the tank with 100ppm Bleach Sanitizer.
  6. Let the tank air dry before using it again.

Important Warnings

⚠️ It is very important to tighten the red cap when removing the waste water tank.

⚠️ It is also very important to loosen the red cap when reinstalling the waste water tank.

⚠️ Do NOT spill waste water — carry tank carefully

⚠️ Make sure waste water tube is inside the tank before closing cabinet

⚠️ Report damaged tank or leaking immediately
