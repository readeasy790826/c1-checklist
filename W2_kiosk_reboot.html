W2 — Kiosk Reboot

Weekly
Purpose: Clear memory buildup and prevent the kiosk app from freezing or becoming unresponsive over time.

Steps

  1. Step 1 — Power off the kiosk Unplug the kiosk power cable completely.
  2. Step 2 — Wait 30 seconds Leave it unpowered for at least 30 seconds to fully discharge.
  3. Step 3 — Power on the kiosk Plug the power cable back in and wait for the kiosk to fully boot up.
  4. Step 4 — Restart the kiosk app Once the kiosk has booted, relaunch / restart the kiosk app and confirm it loads correctly.
⚠️ Note: Make sure the kiosk app is fully loaded and displaying correctly before leaving.
